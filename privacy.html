Introduction

Hazelle Tarot (“Hazelle Tarot”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Hazelle Tarot mobile application and related services.

By downloading or using Hazelle Tarot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Journal entries and saved tarot readings
  • Personal notes added to readings
  • Subscription selections
  • Support requests or feedback
  • App preferences and settings

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device model and operating system
  • App version
  • Language and region settings
  • Anonymous usage analytics
  • Crash and performance reports
  • Subscription status and entitlement validation

This information is used to improve app performance, stability, and user experience.

AI-Generated Interpretations

Hazelle Tarot may use artificial intelligence services to generate tarot interpretations and reflective guidance.

AI-generated content is provided solely for entertainment, self-reflection, mindfulness, and spiritual inspiration.

AI-generated responses should not be relied upon as professional advice of any kind.

  • Medical advice
  • Psychological treatment
  • Legal advice
  • Financial advice
  • Emergency services
  • Professional counselling

Users remain fully responsible for their personal choices, decisions, and actions.

Subscriptions & Payments

Hazelle Tarot offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions.

Payments are securely processed through Apple App Store services. Hazelle Tarot does not directly collect, process, or store payment card information.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

Users may manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple Account Settings.

Restore Purchases functionality is available within the application.

Data Storage & Security

Saved readings, journal entries, and app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Where Apple cloud services are enabled by the user, certain information may also sync through Apple-managed infrastructure.

We implement reasonable safeguards designed to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Analytics & Third-Party Services

Hazelle Tarot may use third-party services including:

  • Apple App Store
  • StoreKit
  • Analytics providers
  • Crash reporting services
  • AI processing providers

Hazelle Tarot does not sell personal information to third parties.

International Users

Hazelle Tarot may be accessed globally. By using the app, you understand that information may be processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Children’s Privacy

Hazelle Tarot is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including:

  • Accessing your data
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of personal information
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Updated versions will be posted with a revised “Last updated” date.
